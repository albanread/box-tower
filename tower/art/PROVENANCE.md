# Where the pictures came from

Pictures marked with an upstream file are derived from tower-together
(github.com/phulin/tower-together, `apps/client/public/rooms/`, MIT, Copyright
Patrick Hulin; the licence is in `../UPSTREAM-LICENSE.md`).  Each derived file
begins with a comment naming its source and what was changed.  Everything else
was drawn for this port.  Nothing here is traced from, copied from or modelled
on the 1994 game, its sequels or any other game, or taken from the web.

Night layers (`*.emit.svg`): for every derived room in use, the emit layer
refers to the day picture under warm light (see STYLE.md); the metro's and
the cathedral's are drawn by hand for this port.  Vacant rooms and
`parking.no-access` have none.

Not taken: upstream's `for-rent.svg` and `for-sale.svg` (banners lettered in
Arial; the program draws the label itself), `stairs.svg` and `escalator.svg`
(transport), `sim-*.svg` (people), and the `.png`/`.jpg` references in that
folder.

Other directories (`src/transport`, `src/people`, `src/building`, `src/world`,
`src/events`, `src/vehicles`) are listed by the artists who drew them below.
